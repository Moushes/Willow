How to Use
├ 1. Go to "Activate" menu and toggle "ON/OFF" to activate PCS.
└ 2. Select any sound option you want to use.

Expression Menu Explaining
│
[Activate] (Submenu)
│
[ON/OFF]
├ This is the main enablement of PCS. Disabling this option means completely shutting down PCS.
│
[Oral, Boobs, Vaginal, Anal]
├ Here are the audio options. Only one item can be selected at a time. (You might ask why there can't be multiple sounds at the same time, that's because VRChat limits audio to 3 instances.)
│
[Custom]
├ This option will cause the audio position to change to the position you set. You must select the sound you want to use while turning on this option.
│
[Auto-mode]
└ Using this mode will automatically turn on/off each sound option for you. Depends on the location where it was inserted, the first penetrator will be prioritized.

[Satisfaction] (Submenu)
│
[Mute - Moan]
├ Mute all moaning sounds.
│
[Squirt on Insert]
├ Makes every insertion creates squirt particles. This option will turn on automatically when you are about to orgasm.
│
[Force Orgasm]
├ Instantly fill up your Lust Points and make your avatar reach orgasm.
│
[Reset]
└ Reset all Lust Points and states.

[Edging (Pause Events)]
├ Temporary freeze Lust Points so you won't gain any during insertion. This is great when you only want to hear Random Moans but not Event Moans (long moan).
│
[Mute - Smash]
├ Mute all smash hit sounds.
│
[Self-service]
└ This mode allows you to trigger sounds based on your own movements, regardless of the Penetrator. Work best in the situation that your partner doesn't have a penetrator installed or you are on the top.
